For employers
What exactly does tulifo do?

It runs your whole interview process autonomously. You publish a role; every applicant is screened against the JD, interviewed on video with adaptive follow-ups, identity-verified, scored, and reported – without your team touching the pipeline.

Do I need an ATS?

No. Every role publishes to a careers page with your logo and name, candidates apply there, and the pipeline runs inside tulifo end to end.

How fast do I get results?

The scored report lands one second after the interview ends. From application to report is about sixteen minutes – most of it the interview itself.

How does fraud detection work?

Identity is verified against records before the first question; proctoring runs throughout for second voices, second screens, and scripted reading. Every flag is cited with its timestamp in the report – your team makes the final call, never the machine.

Can I control when interviews happen?

Yes. Interview candidates immediately on application, schedule them, or hold them at the match score until a trigger you define – manual or automatic – fires.

What does it cost?

You pay per interview – no seats, no platform fee. See pricing for current rates; credits don't expire.

Data & billing
Where is my data stored?

In India, retained for the window the employer sets, and deletable on request – see the privacy policy.

How do refunds work?

Unused credits are refundable within 30 days; delivered interviews aren't; failures on our side are re-credited automatically. Full rules in the refund policy.
