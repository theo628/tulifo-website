Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and who sees it. No surprises – that's the whole policy.

Effective 19 July 2026 · Tulifo Private Limited

Who we are

tulifo is an AI interview platform operated by Tulifo Private Limited, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India ("Tulifo", "we"). This policy covers the tulifo platform and this website.

What we collect

  • From employers: account details, company information, job descriptions, and billing information.
  • From candidates: name, contact details, resume, interview video and audio, transcripts, skill-task work, scores, and identity-verification signals.
  • From this website: nothing beyond standard server logs. We run no advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

One purpose: running the hiring pipeline. Screening resumes, conducting and scoring interviews, verifying identity, and delivering reports to the employer whose role you applied for. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use candidate data for advertising.

Who sees candidate data

Your interview, scores, and report go to one place: the employer whose role you applied for. Not to other employers, not to data brokers, not to anyone else. Service providers who help us run the platform (hosting, payments) process data under contract and only on our instructions.

Where it lives

Candidate recordings, transcripts, and scores are stored in India and protected with industry-standard safeguards, encrypted in transit.

How long we keep it

Interview data is retained for the window the employer sets on their account, then deleted. You can request deletion of your data sooner at any time – see your rights below.

Your rights in India · DPDP Act, 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your data.
  • Raise a grievance about how your data is handled – it goes to our grievance officer.

Write to privacy@tulifo.ai. We respond within 30 days.

Your rights in the EU/EEA · GDPR

Where the GDPR applies to you: for candidate interview data, the employer you applied to is the data controller and Tulifo Private Limited is the processor, acting on the employer's documented instructions. For account and billing data, we are the controller.

  • Legal bases: performance of a contract, legitimate interests in running the platform, and consent where required.
  • You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing – write to privacy@tulifo.ai.
  • Data is stored in India; transfers from the EU/EEA are safeguarded by standard contractual clauses.
  • You can lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority at any time.

Automated scoring supports the employer's decision; it is not an automated rejection. You can ask the employer for human review of any decision about your application.

A note on verification

Identity checks and proctoring exist to protect candidates too – they stop someone else from interviewing in your name. Flags are always reviewed by the employer's human team; they are never silent, automatic rejections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Material changes are notified to account holders by email.
