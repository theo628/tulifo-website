About

The first chapter
shouldn't be paper.

A resume is a claim. An interview is evidence. Tulifo exists because the first is deciding careers that the second never got a chance to.

01 · Access

Every candidate gets interviewed

Not the fifty whose resumes survived a keyword filter – everyone who applies. An autonomous interviewer makes that affordable for the first time.

02 · Evidence

Decisions on evidence, not gut feel

Skills shown on camera, one scoring scale across every candidate, and a transcript behind every number. If a score can't be traced, it shouldn't count.

03 · Autonomy

Recruiters should decide, not administer

Scheduling, screening, chasing, re-scheduling – none of it is judgment. We automate the admin end to end so the human hours go where they matter: the hire.
