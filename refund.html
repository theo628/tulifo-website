Cancellation &
refund policy.

Simple and by the book: payments are non-refundable except in the few cases below – and since credits never expire, nothing you've paid for is ever lost.

Effective 19 July 2026 · Tulifo Private Limited

The model

Employers buy interview credits and spend one per interview. Credits don't expire. Candidates never pay anything, so there's nothing for a candidate to refund.

The rule

Except as expressly provided in this policy or where required by law, all payments are non-refundable. Unused credits are not refunded – they simply stay on your account and never expire, ready for your next role.

Cancellation

There is no subscription and no lock-in – you can stop purchasing at any time, and cancelling costs nothing. Cancelling does not refund payments already made; credits you've bought remain available until used. To close your account, email support@tulifo.ai.

The exceptions – what we do refund

  • Interviews that failed because of a fault on our side – the credit is re-issued automatically, or refunded on request.
  • Duplicate or erroneous charges – refunded in full.
  • If we close your account for any reason other than a breach of the terms – unused credits refunded in full.
  • Anything the law requires us to refund.

What we don't refund

  • Completed interviews. A delivered interview and report is a delivered service – whether the candidate was hired, screened out, or flagged.
  • Interviews a candidate abandoned or failed for reasons on their side (connection, device, no-show).
  • Unused credits – they don't expire and stay spendable on your account.
  • Accounts terminated for a breach of the terms.

How to ask

Email support@tulifo.ai from your account email with the invoice or order reference. We confirm within 2 business days, and approved refunds reach your original payment method within 5–7 business days. GST on refunded amounts is adjusted per the credit note.

Enterprise agreements

If you're on a custom enterprise contract, its refund terms apply instead of this page.
