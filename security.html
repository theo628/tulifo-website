Security

Found something?
Tell us first.

If you believe you've found a vulnerability in tulifo, we want to hear it from you before anyone else does. We read every report, and good-faith research is welcome here.

Responsible disclosure · Tulifo Private Limited

How to report

Email security@tulifo.ai. No forms, no portal – a plain email with the details below reaches the people who can fix it.

What to include

  • The page or endpoint you believe is affected.
  • What you observed, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • Numbered steps to reproduce it – a short video or screenshots help a lot.

What happens next

We acknowledge your report within 2 business days, keep you posted while we investigate, and reach out if we need more detail. When the issue is fixed, you hear that too.

Disclosure

To protect employers and candidates, we don't discuss or confirm a security issue until the investigation is complete and fixes are live. We ask you to hold public disclosure until then – and we're glad to coordinate timing with you.

Good-faith research

We will not pursue legal action over good-faith security research. Good faith means:

  • If you reach data that isn't yours – a candidate's interview, another employer's pipeline – stop and report. Don't read further, copy, or share it.
  • Don't degrade the service: no denial-of-service testing, no spam, nothing that interrupts a live interview.
  • No social engineering or phishing of our team, employers, or candidates.

Out of scope

  • Automated scanner output without a working proof of concept.
  • Missing best-practice headers with no demonstrated impact.
  • Clickjacking on pages with no sensitive action.
