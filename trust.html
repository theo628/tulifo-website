Trust

Trust, in
plain terms.

Interviews are careers and candidates are people – the data they hand us deserves better than boilerplate. Here is how we protect it, stated the way we score: with evidence, not adjectives.

Residency

Data stays in India

Candidate recordings, transcripts, and scores are stored in India, retained for the window the employer sets, then deleted.

Encryption

Encrypted in transit

Everything moving between candidates, employers, and tulifo travels encrypted, protected with industry-standard safeguards.

Access

One employer sees it

A candidate's interview goes to the employer whose role they applied for. Not to other employers, not to data brokers, not to anyone else.

Retention

You set the window

Retention is an account setting, not a support ticket. When the window closes, the data goes – and deletion requests are honored sooner.

Rights

Candidate rights, honored

Access, correction, deletion, and grievance under India's DPDP Act, 2023 – and GDPR rights for EU/EEA candidates. The privacy policy has the full list.

Judgment

Flags, never verdicts

Integrity flags cite their timestamp and evidence in the report. A human on the hiring team makes every call – never the machine, never silently.

The honest part

Certifications

We don't claim what we don't hold. No SOC 2 or ISO badge appears on this page until an auditor has said so – and when audits complete, this is where they'll land.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment gateway. Card numbers never touch our servers – we see that you paid, not what you paid with.

If something goes wrong

If an incident affects your data, we tell you – account holders are notified by email, quickly and completely. No quiet fixes.

Diligence & questionnaires

Running a vendor review? Send security questionnaires to support@tulifo.ai – we respond within one business day.
